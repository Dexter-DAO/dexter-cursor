OpenDexter

Connect your wallet
to your terminal.

Your Dexter wallet lives behind your passkey. Let the local OpenDexter package show its balance and Solana deposit address in under a minute. No keys copied. No passwords.

You need: the OpenDexter tool and: a Dexter wallet from dexter.cash time: ~1 minute
  1. Install OpenDexter
    terminal
    $ npm install -g @dexterai/opendexter

    A one-time install. Don't want a global install? Run commands with npx @dexterai/opendexter@latest … instead. Both paths require Node.js.

  2. Run connect
    terminal
    $ opendexter connect

── Connect your Dexter wallet ──────────────
Approve with your passkey — any one of these:

  1. Open this link
  2. Or scan the QR code
  3. Or enter code PTXF-HHXQ at dexter.cash/wallet/connect

Waiting for approval…

    Your terminal offers three ways in. You only need one.

  3. Approve with your passkey
    • LaptopClick the link — your browser opens the approval page.
    • PhoneScan the QR code with your camera.
    • ServerNo browser? Type the short code at dexter.cash/wallet/connect on any device.

    The page shows which app is asking. Tap Face ID or your fingerprint. That's the only thing that approves — and it never leaves your device.

  4. See your wallet
    terminal
    $ opendexter wallet

Dexter wallet
  lane: connected

  USDC balance
    $0.00

  Deposit address
    3NWeBKn…QR9Ke

    Your real balance and deposit address, right in the terminal. Done.

That's the whole thing.

Your keysNo private key is copied into the terminal. Your passkey approves the connection — no password or secret key to paste.
Payment signerConnect does not change the payment signer. Local paid calls still use the local wallet or configured environment keys.
Your controlRevoke the connector at dexter.cash/wallet. opendexter connect disconnect only removes this terminal's stored session.
AnywhereLaptop or a headless server over SSH — the short-code path always works when there's no browser.
To fund itSend USDC on Solana to the deposit address opendexter wallet shows you. Always that one.
